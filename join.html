How to Join

We're an incorporated club affiliated with ACCSA, running under a formal Constitution — but joining in is a lot more relaxed than that sounds.

1

Come along as a visitor

Prospective members are welcome to attend up to three rallies within a six-month period as a visitor, before joining formally — a genuine no-pressure way to see if the club's the right fit for you.

2

Get to know everyone

Happy hours, shared meals, and campfire chats are where most of the real "joining in" happens — turn up, say hello, and you'll be part of the group before you know it.

3

Apply for membership

When you're ready, submit an application. New memberships are considered and welcomed at our Annual General Meeting, held each September.

4

You're in!

Membership is capped at 30 vans under our Constitution, so once we're full a waiting list applies — get in early.
